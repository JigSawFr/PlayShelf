### 🌟 Highlights

The first release of PlayShelf: the settings of Nintendo's Parental Controls phone app, on the console itself, offline. Custom firmware (Atmosphère) required.

* ⏱️ **Play-time limit**: the same every day or one per day on a week chart, saved profiles, extra time today in one press, and a one-switch console lock.
* 📊 **Activity**: play time per game and per user account, day by day, a summary per period, and export to CSV, JSON, XLSX or PDF.
* 🔒 **Restrictions and PIN**: restriction level, age rating and its organisation, show or change the PIN, and an optional PIN to open PlayShelf.
* 🕒 **Network clock**: set from public NTP servers, so the play timer counts right on a console that never reaches Nintendo.
* 🛡️ **Safe by design**: the play timer is never written while it counts down, every change is logged and can be undone, settings are backed up to the SD card.
* 🛟 **A way back in**: a recovery sysmodule for a forgotten PIN, and unlinking the phone app on a second-hand console.
* 🔄 **Updates and reports**: update in place from PlayShelf, "What's new" after each update, diagnostic reports sent as a link and QR codes.
* 🌍 **Languages and themes**: every console language, light and dark themes.

Coming from Pctl Manager: the app is now in `sd:/switch/playshelf/` and its files in `sd:/config/playshelf/`.
